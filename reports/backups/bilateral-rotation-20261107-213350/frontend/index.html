    Metrics guide

    Markout B/A
    Average price move after a fill on the bid and ask sides. Positive markout means fills are followed by favorable price movement.
    Required edge / side
    Minimum half-spread the engine needs before quoting a side, compared with the current quoted half-spread.
    Cost / 1M
    All-in cost per million dollars of volume: fees minus realized PnL, normalized by volume.
    Fills/h
    Number of fills per hour since the session started.
    Actions/h
    Order placements, modifications and cancellations per hour.
    Spread
    Current quoted spread in basis points.
    Position
    Current position notional in USD, including unrealized exposure.
