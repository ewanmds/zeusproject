Strategy

1 bps = 0.01%. This is the distance on each side of mid, not the total spread. Tick rounding can make small settings produce the same prices. A larger distance also increases the opening-order reprice step: 25% of the distance, at least one tick. In normal mode, reduce-only exits rest at break-even; risk actions stay post-only. Changes apply only to new sessions.

Use entry price, observed fees and one tick of margin. Normal MID mode keeps the reduce-only exit at this break-even anchor until the market comes back; ordinary stop/max-hold loss exits are off (OBI_MID_LOSS_CUTS=1 restores them). A catastrophic loss guard remains; holding a position longer can increase losses.

BBO/MID automatic orders stay post-only, including risk exits. Manual Stop may close at market. If an automatic stop or a disconnected exchange leaves a position open, its closure remains unconfirmed.

Metrics guide

Markout B/A
Average price move after a fill on the bid and ask sides. Positive markout means fills are followed by favorable price movement.
Required edge / side
Minimum half-spread the engine needs before quoting a side, compared with the current quoted half-spread.
Cost / 1M
All-in cost per million dollars of volume: fees minus realized PnL, normalized by volume.
Fills/h
Number of fills per hour since the session started.
Actions/h
Order placements, modifications and cancellations per hour.
Spread
Current quoted spread in basis points.
Position
Current position notional in USD, including unrealized exposure.
